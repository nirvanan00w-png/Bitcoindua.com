2014

I was starting a business to buy and sell bitcoin, and it needed a name. The word that came to me was from my grandmother's language. Dua. Tree.

I joined it to bitcoin and registered the domain. Namecheap took bitcoin in those days, so that is how I paid.

2016

Bitcoindua bought and sold bitcoin in Ghana. From June to August of 2016, one trader and I moved more than $60,000 through it. There were other traders too.

I treated bitcoin as money to move. The name on the door said to plant it.

2019

The bear market of 2018 closed the business, and I let the domain lapse.

One morning in 2021, with bitcoin near its high, I typed the address in again. The name was for sale. The asking price was $99,999.

2024

In September I found the name free again, and I took it back.

By then, public companies were holding bitcoin in their treasuries and talking about it in decades. A tree is planted by people who expect to wait. The right word had been there for ten years.

The next ring

It belongs to whoever plants here next. The name would suit a treasury company or a fund with a long horizon. It would suit an African exchange just as well.

It was first bought with bitcoin, and I would like to sell it for bitcoin.
